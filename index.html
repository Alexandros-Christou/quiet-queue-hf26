A quieter way through the list

Quiet Queue

Drop in the things you keep putting off. Get small batches of similar work, with every original task kept intact.

This local prototype suggests Reply, Read or Arrange. Unfamiliar tasks go to Review. It never sends messages, changes a calendar or deletes anything.


Review the suggestions before using them. The small model was trained on 24 synthetic examples, not your inbox. Text stays in this local process and is not saved.
